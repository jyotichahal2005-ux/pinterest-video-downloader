How to Download Pinterest Videos with PinSaver

The same three moves work on your phone or your computer — copy, paste, download. Here is exactly what to tap on every device.

📱 From the Pinterest mobile app

  1. Open the video pin and tap the share (arrow) icon. On the latest Pinterest app, it sits at the bottom right of the screen.
  2. Tap Copy Link. Pinterest gives you a short pin.it/... link — that's exactly what we need.
  3. Paste the link into the PinSaver box above and tap Download.
  4. See the preview, choose your quality (480p, 720p or 1080p where available) and tap the download button.
  5. The MP4 lands in your Files app (iPhone) or Downloads / Gallery (Android).

💻 From a desktop browser

  1. Open the pin on pinterest.com and copy the URL from your address bar — it looks like pinterest.com/pin/1234567890/.
  2. Paste the link into the PinSaver box and press Download (or press Enter).
  3. A preview appears with the pin title, thumbnail, and every video quality Pinterest actually serves for that pin.
  4. Pick your quality and the file saves to your Downloads folder in a few seconds.
  5. Drag it anywhere you like — or check Ctrl+J to open your download history.
