Native apps and extensions

Desktop apps are the older-school option. They can work well for heavy bulk saving, but they ask for file-system permissions, need updates, and often gate original quality behind a paid tier. Browser extensions are convenient until Pinterest changes its page markup — extensions then quietly break and sit on your install list doing nothing. For occasional use, extensions also add a small privacy burden: they run inside the page you’re already on. All of these still rely on the same public CDN that browser tools use, so there’s no quality advantage to compensate.

Skips you can skip

  • Avoid anything that promises to “unlock private boards” — that’s both unsafe and against the spirit of how Pinterest works.
  • Skip tools that dump ads between you and the download button.
  • Be wary of any that require an account just to export a single video.
  • When a free tool needs your email, it usually has a different plan for you.
