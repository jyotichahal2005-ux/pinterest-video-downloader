iPhone & iPad guide

Pinterest Video Downloader for iPhone & iPad.

You don’t need an App Store app to save Pinterest videos on your iPhone. PinSaver works right in Safari — copy a pin link, paste it, and the MP4 lands in your Files app, ready to move to Photos.

The App Store doesn’t allow generic Pinterest video downloaders, which is exactly why browser-based tools are the smart choice on iOS. Nothing gets installed, nothing sits in your settings, and nothing touches your iCloud. You stay logged into Pinterest as usual, and PinSaver only ever sees the link you paste.

How to download a Pinterest video on iPhone

1

Copy the pin link

Open Pinterest, tap the video pin, tap Share, then choose Copy Link.

2

Open PinSaver in Safari

Go to pinsaver.app from the same device — no app required.

3

Paste and click Download

The tool sniffs out every available quality and shows the MP4 options.

4

Choose your quality

Pick 480p, 720p or 1080p when available, then tap the download arrow in Safari.

5

Save the video

The file appears in Downloads. Tap it and choose Save Video to move it into your Photos library.

What to know about the Files app

On iPhone and iPad the downloaded MP4 is stored in the Downloads folder inside the Files app. From there you can share it anywhere — WhatsApp, iMessage, AirDrop, or straight to your camera roll via the share sheet.

Tip: If a Safari prompt asks for permission to download, just allow it; the video only saves where you tell it to.

Does it work with long pins and mixed boards?

Yes. PinSaver handles standard and long (vertical) video pins. If a pin contains only an image there is nothing to download — and no downloader should silently guess. For videos, you always get the original file, untouched, in MP4 format that every iOS app understands.

Staying private while you save

The tool never asks you to hand over your Pinterest username, and it never stores the videos you download. Each paste is handled as a fresh request, so your history stays on your own phone. That makes PinSaver a comfortable option even if you use a shared iPad or a work tablet.

A few seconds, not a trailer

Long videos on Pinterest can be surprisingly big, but downloads still finish quickly on Wi-Fi and 5G. You can keep browsing Pinterest normally while the file saves in the background, because the download runs in a separate tab. When the MP4 appears in Files, you’ll recognize it instantly by the pin thumbnail — rename it if you like.
