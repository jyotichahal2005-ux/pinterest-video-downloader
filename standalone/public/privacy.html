Plain-language privacy

Your link is not your profile.

This policy explains what PinSaver handles when you use the downloader and what we intentionally do not ask for.

What we receive

When you submit a Pinterest URL, PinSaver needs that link to attempt to read the public pin and find available video files. We do not ask you to create an account, provide a name, or submit a password.

What we do not store

PinSaver is not designed as a social profile, personal archive, or cloud storage. We do not store your downloaded videos on our servers, and we do not keep a personal download history. The resulting download is handled directly by your own browser and saved to your own device.

No user accounts

Because PinSaver requires no login, there is no account database containing your personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, or profile data through the download process.

Analytics

PinSaver is a static tool that does not currently include third-party analytics or tracking scripts. No visitor IDs, fingerprinting, or behavioral tracking are used. If we add basic, aggregate analytics in the future to understand how many downloads happen or which pages are visited, we will update this page and keep any data collected in an aggregated, non-identifying form.

Public content and third parties

To resolve a link, the service may request information from public Pinterest pages or related video delivery systems. Those services have their own policies, which you should review. PinSaver cannot control what Pinterest or a source host does with a request.

Local browser details

Your browser may keep standard technical information such as cached files, local history, or downloads. You control those settings through your browser. We recommend keeping your browser and device updated.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, contact hello@pinsaver.app. This plain-language summary is not legal advice and may be updated as the service changes.
