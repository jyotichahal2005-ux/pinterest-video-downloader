Pinterest Video Downloader for Android.

Android is the easiest place to use PinSaver. Open Chrome (or any browser), paste a Pinterest pin link, and the MP4 downloads straight into your Downloads folder — which shows up in your Gallery automatically.

There are dozens of Pinterest downloader apps on the Play Store, but most are crammed with ads, request sketchy permissions, or quietly add a watermark. A browser-based downloader skips all of that: no APK, no permissions, no background service chewing your battery. Just a link, a download, and your video.

How to save a Pinterest video on Android

1

Copy the pin link

Open the video pin, tap the Share icon and choose Copy Link.

2

Paste it into Chrome

Open https://pinterest-video-downloader-69a6.onrender.com and drop the link into the downloader box.

3

Tap Download

Pick your preferred quality — 480p, 720p or 1080p where it exists.

4

Allow the download

If Chrome asks for download permission, say allow.

5

Check your Gallery

The MP4 lands in Downloads and your Gallery picks it up automatically. You can also find it under Files > Downloads.

Why the file appears in Gallery instantly

Android’s media scanner detects new MP4 files in the Downloads folder and indexes them into the gallery within seconds.

Tip: If your Gallery app is being slow, open the Files app, navigate to Downloads, and play the video directly — it is already there.

Works on all Android browsers

Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet and Microsoft Edge are all supported. The tool needs nothing from the operating system, so it behaves identically on phones and Android tablets. Long video pins, vertical pins and regular pins all download as a single untouched MP4 file.

What about background and Chromecast?

Once the MP4 is in your Gallery, it behaves like any video you own. You can share it to WhatsApp and Telegram, upload it to Drive, or cast it to a Chromecast on your TV. Because PinSaver saves the original file rather than a re-encoded copy, it also keeps audio in sync with video on every player — no codec surprises on budget Android devices.

Privacy on Android

The tool requires zero permissions and zero account setup, so there are no contacts, storage or microphone permissions to grant. Your Pinterest account stays untouched and the app you “didn’t install” can’t sneak background access later. That’s the advantage of keeping your saving workflow in the browser.
