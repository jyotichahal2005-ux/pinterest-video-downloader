Mac guide

Pinterest Video Downloader for Mac.

Mac users can save Pinterest videos without installing anything. PinSaver runs in Safari or Chrome, fetches the original MP4, and your file is on disk in seconds — ready to drop into Photos, QuickTime or an editing app.

A clean Mac setup doesn’t have room for ad-heavy downloader apps. Browser-based saving keeps your Applications folder empty: you copy a link, paste it, download, and the work is done in one tab. It also respects your privacy — PinSaver never sees your Pinterest password or accounts, only the URL you paste.

How to download a Pinterest video on Mac

1

Copy the pin link

Use the Pin’s share menu (Copy Link) or copy the URL from Safari’s address bar.

2

Open PinSaver in your browser

Any of Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Arc work fine.

3

Paste, pick quality, download

Choose 480p, 720p or 1080p where available. Safari shows a small download icon in the toolbar while it works.

4

Locate the MP4

By default it goes to ~/Downloads. Double-click to play, or drag it into the Photos app to keep it in your library with everything else.

Nice touches for macOS

Because Chrome on Mac keeps downloads in one place, you’ll find every saved pin neatly in the Downloads stack on your Dock. You can even drag the file into an iMessage or Slack chat to share it directly.

Tip: Use Quick Look (press the space bar) to preview the video without opening an app. And with AirPlay, anything you save to Photos can be streamed straight to your TV.

Original quality, every time

There’s no re-encoding on a Mac with PinSaver. The file you get is the same MP4 Pinterest serves — no logo, no compressed second pass, no surprise file size. Public video pins only, personal use, and completely free.

Organising your library

Saved MP4s drop neatly into Downloads, where macOS gives you Quick Look, Spotlight search and iCloud Drive at no extra cost. Drop the file into a folder for your favourite boards, or add it to Apple’s Photos so it shows up on every device that shares your iCloud library. The file is standard MP4, so no converter or codec pack is ever required on macOS.

Compatibility notes

PinSaver works on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs alike, with Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Arc. There’s no helper application, kernel extension or storage permission to manage — which means nothing to uninstall later either. The entire workflow stays inside one tab.
