Images on Pinterest

Pinterest Image Downloader.

Pinterest is packed with reference images — palettes, room inspo, recipes, fashion. Saving them is simple on every device, and full image-download support is coming to PinSaver shortly.

Save Pinterest images today

Full one-click image downloads in PinSaver are on the way and will match the tool’s video behavior: paste a link, pick the size, save. Until that update arrives, the built-in Pinterest flows below are the fastest way to keep any image:

  • iPhone & iPad: long-press the image and choose Save Image — it goes straight to Photos.
  • Android: long-press the image and tap Download image. It lands in your Gallery.
  • PC & Mac: right-click the image and choose Save image as…. You can pick any folder and rename the file as you like.

This preserves the image at the resolution Pinterest displays, which is exactly what most reference boards need.

When to use the video downloader for an “image” pin

Some pins look like a static image but are actually a video thumbnail — the pin plays a looping video when you open it. For those, paste the link into the PinSaver video downloader and save the full MP4 instead.

Tip: If the pin is a true photo, nothing will download; that’s the correct behavior, and the tool tells you so honestly rather than returning a broken file.

Keeping references organised

Images you save long-press style keep their exact timestamp, which makes them easy to bullet through later. Renaming with the board name before saving keeps a tidy reference library — “bathroom-inspo.jpg” beats “unnamed pin 42.” Nothing about the process needs an account, so your Pinterest history stays private and your downloads folder stays clean.

What “full image support” will look like

When image downloads ship, pasting an image pin into PinSaver will offer the source-size JPG (and WebP where Pinterest stores it), downloaded in one click without right-click tricks. The feature will be free, watermark-free and subject to the same public-only, personal-use rules that keep the video tool honest.

The roadmap in plain words

GIF support lands first, image downloads follow. When they ship, pasting an image pin will return the source-size JPG with the same simplicity, privacy and watermark-free rules as the video tool — free and unlimited.
